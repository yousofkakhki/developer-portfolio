# Yousef Kakhki — Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea

**Systems Backbone**

The monogram combines the initials **Y** and **K** through one shared architectural spine. The spine quietly suggests the numeral **1**, representing focus, ownership, reliability, and technical leadership. The teal node marks the decision point where architecture is defined and systems connect. A single signal line represents controlled information flow.

## Positioning

**Yousef Kakhki**

**System Architect & Technical Lead**

Primary expertise line:

`Distributed Systems • Real-Time Platforms • AI Systems • Infrastructure`

## Logo hierarchy

1. The viewer should first read **YK**.
2. The shared backbone and hidden **1** are secondary discoveries.
3. The node and flow line express systems architecture and controlled execution.

## Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the width of the central spine around all sides of the mark. Do not allow text, imagery, or other logos to enter this zone.

## Minimum size

- Full lockup: minimum 320 px wide digitally.
- Primary symbol: minimum 64 px.
- Micro icon: minimum 16 px.
- At sizes below 48 px, use the micro icon without the flow line.

## Color palette

- Deep Navy — `#071018`
- Surface Navy — `#101B27`
- Active Teal — `#16F2B3`
- Deep Teal — `#0A7B68`
- Silver Light — `#F3F6F8`
- Silver Mid — `#A7ADB5`
- Silver Dark — `#4C5560`
- Pure White — `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

Recommended:

- Headings and name: **Sora** or **Manrope**
- Interface and body copy: **Inter**
- Technical annotations: **IBM Plex Mono**
- Persian: **Vazirmatn**

Font files are not included. Use licensed or web-hosted versions from the relevant provider.

## Usage

Use the primary metallic version for hero graphics, presentation covers, and cinematic applications. Use the flat version for UI, web navigation, résumé headers, and normal digital use. Use monochrome versions for printing, stamping, embroidery, and constrained production.

## Avoid

- Do not change the angle of individual branches.
- Do not enlarge the node until it becomes the first thing the eye sees.
- Do not add binary digits, circuit-board textures, AI brains, or cloud icons to the static master logo.
- Do not use glow effects in the flat or monochrome versions.
- Do not compress, stretch, rotate, or recolor the mark outside the approved palette.

## Motion direction

For animation, assemble the logo in this order:

1. Backbone rises.
2. Left branch forms the Y.
3. Upper-right branch completes the Y and begins the K.
4. Lower-right branch locks into the K.
5. Node activates.
6. The signal line moves once, then the logo becomes static.

The website micro-animation should last approximately 1.2–1.8 seconds and should not loop continuously.
